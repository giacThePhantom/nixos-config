# Dashboard

There is no bar at the top of the screen. Everything a bar would show lives
in the dashboard: panels that open in the middle of the screen when you call
them, and close when you're done. While they're hidden they cost nothing.

`Super + A` opens the menu; press a panel's letter to open it, or use its own
key directly. While a panel is open, another panel's letter switches to it,
and `Esc` closes.

## Calendar: `Super + C`

The time, the month, and your events for the next seven days. Click an event
to open it in renCal, the calendar app, where you connect your calendars
(Google, Microsoft, CalDAV) and add events. Find renCal in the launcher.

## Media: `Super + S`

What's playing (Spotify, Firefox, mpv... anything that reports it), with
play/pause, previous and next. Below it the volume and mute, the output
device, and a microphone mute button. *Mixer* opens the full volume mixer,
where you also choose which speakers or headphones to use.

## System: `Super + I`

Processor, memory and disk use, your network and IP address (on a laptop,
also the battery and the power mode), the kernel, and the system's version
number (it goes up at every `rebuild`). *Open monitor*
opens btop, for the full picture.

## Power: `Super + Shift + E`

Lock, suspend, log out, reboot, shut down, plus how long the computer has
been on.
